Project Validation

Joe Cowles, an HPC researcher at the TTU Lab and one of Lexeme’s target users, tested the product locally with the large OpenSHMEM MLIR codebase. He found Lexeme easy to use, its navigation intuitive, and its visualizations accurate.

His feedback also revealed opportunities to make switching between graph views and reading graph layouts easier. We responded by adding a right-click “go to function” option and integrating the Eclipse Layout Kernel for cleaner, more logical layouts. Joe considered Lexeme an indispensable tool for understanding and navigating unfamiliar C11 codebases.
