Stage 1 Goals and Meeting Activities

  • Identify target users and gather requirements through interviews with potential users.
  • Review interview findings and define the system's functional, data, and performance requirements.
  • Develop the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) based on IEEE Std 830-1998.
  • Define the initial project scope, major features, and planned system modules.
  • Use team meetings to assign responsibilities, review progress, resolve open issues, and prepare the Stage 1 report and presentation.
